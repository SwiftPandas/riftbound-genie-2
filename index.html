How to read these numbers

50% (dashed line) is an even matchup. With 100 games a result can swing about 5 points either way, with 20 games about 11; use 250+ fast games before changing real cards. Look-ahead plays closer to how good players play, so use it to double-check a close call. The bars and the big number are real-world adjusted. In the engine both players are equally good, so the deck decides more games than in real events, where players of different skill meet. Each matchup is scaled by a skill effect fitted to real post-ban results (Open the Vault $5k and MFG's Riftbound $2k, 70 players, riftbound.gg): realistic = 50% + 0.6 × (engine − 50%), plus a small per-legend correction. Real records are best-of-3 matches, so they're turned into the game win rate that produces them before fitting; every number here is a win rate per game. The fit's own games are played best of 3 too: game 1 with the main decks, games 2 and 3 with the meta decks' real sideboards swapped in where they help. The plain engine number is under the big number and in each bar's tooltip; use it to compare versions of the same deck. Meta decks are real post-ban lists for Vex, Master Yi, Kai'Sa, Azir, Irelia, Evelynn, Sett, Ezreal and Rek'Sai; Kennen, Rengar, LeBlanc, Akali and Ornn are their newest pre-ban lists with banned cards swapped.

Match settings

Final-point rule: your last point has to come from holding a battlefield, or from a conquer on a turn you've scored every battlefield; otherwise that conquer draws you a card instead. The tracker checks this for you when you score with the battlefield buttons. Each battlefield can only score once per turn.
